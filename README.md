&#x20;Adverse Drug Reaction (ADR) Analysis



&#x20;Project Overview:

This project analyzes pharmaceutical adverse drug reaction data to identify high-risk medicines and understand adverse reaction patterns. The analysis correlates symptoms/adverse reactions with specific drugs and evaluates the severity and frequency of adverse events.



&#x20;Dataset Information:

\- File: `adr\_dataset.csv`

\- Total Medicines/Chemicals Analyzed: 3,772

\- Adverse Reaction Categories: 24 different types

\- Data Format: Binary matrix showing which medicines cause which adverse reactions



&#x20;Adverse Reaction Categories

The dataset includes 24 major adverse reaction categories:

\- Hepatobiliary disorders

\- Metabolism and nutrition disorders

\- Eye disorders

\- Gastrointestinal disorders

\- Cardiac disorders

\- Psychiatric disorders

\- Respiratory disorders

\- Nervous system disorders

\- Immune system disorders

\- Blood and lymphatic system disorders

\- Skin disorders

\- Renal and urinary disorders

\- And 12 more...



&#x20;Analysis Performed



&#x20;1. High-Risk Medicines Identification

Identifies medicines that cause the most adverse reactions, helping to understand which drugs are most problematic.



&#x20;2. Most Common Adverse Reactions

Determines which adverse reactions occur most frequently across the medicine database.



&#x20;3. Medicine Risk Level Distribution

Categorizes medicines into risk levels:

\- Very Low (0-5 adverse reactions)

\- Low (6-10)

\- Medium (11-15)

\- High (16-20)

\- Very High (21+)



&#x20;4. Adverse Reaction Patterns

Analyzes which adverse reactions commonly occur together in high-risk medicines.



&#x20;5. Heatmap Analysis

Visual representation of top 20 high-risk medicines and their adverse reaction profiles.
